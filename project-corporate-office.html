Project information

This 5,000 sq ft corporate office project represents 9M Design Studio's entry into commercial interior design — and it sets a high benchmark. The brief called for a workspace that would do more than simply house employees; it needed to actively express the brand identity of a forward-thinking organisation, foster collaboration and creativity, and provide the focus and calm necessary for high-performance work.

Our team developed a spatial strategy anchored in the distinction between focused work, collaborative exchange, and social connection. The open work floor accommodates over 80 individual workstations within a thoughtfully zoned layout — quiet-focus zones bookending active collaboration hubs. Private executive cabins line the perimeter, benefiting from natural light, while the centrepiece boardroom commands authority with its premium finishes and integrated AV technology. A welcoming reception, a well-designed lounge, and a fully appointed pantry complete this comprehensive workplace design.

Design highlights

This commercial project demonstrates that great office design is not simply functional — it is transformative. A well-designed workspace elevates a brand, attracts talent, and shapes the culture of an organisation from the ground up.

  • Reception designed as a brand statement — with backlit logo wall, premium stone reception desk, and curated welcome greenery.
  • Open work floor with 80+ ergonomic workstations, acoustic panels, and task lighting for individual focus.
  • Dedicated collaboration zones with writable walls, plug-and-play meeting tables, and informal seating clusters.
  • Four private executive cabins with full-height glazing, integrated joinery, and premium desk setups.
  • Premium boardroom seating 14, with motorised projection, video conferencing, and acoustic wall treatment.
  • Employee lounge with breakout seating, espresso bar, and biophilic greenery elements for well-being.
  • Consistent brand palette throughout — charcoal, white, and gold as primary tones, with brand accent colour at key touchpoints.
  • Efficient lighting strategy combining natural light harvesting with layered artificial light for all task needs.
