SHEET A-04 · PROCESS

How we work

From first sketch to final handover.

A clear four-stage method that takes you from brainstorming and conceptualisation through detailed drawings, execution and a thorough final walkthrough.

01SKETCH

initial consultation

A one-on-one meeting to understand your vision, preferences and requirements — site measured, brief written.

022D PLAN

design planning

Layouts, materials, furnishings and lighting drawn in detail, with 3D renderings to preview the finished space.

033D BUILD

project execution

With plans in place we manage and coordinate every aspect of site work, from civil to final finishes.

04HANDOVER

final review

A thorough walkthrough with you to review the finished space, snag-list closed, keys in your hand.
