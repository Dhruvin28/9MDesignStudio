asked question

Have your any question look here now

Our dedicated team works closely to understand your vision and bring it to life with great attention to detail

1. What is the typical process for starting a design project?

We begin with a consultation to understand your vision, style, and budget, followed by concept development and detailed planning. Once finalized, our team manages execution to bring your dream space to life with attention to detail.

2. How long does a typical interior design project take?

Project timelines vary depending on scope, but most interior design projects take anywhere from 4 to 12 weeks. We provide a clear schedule after the initial consultation.

3. Can I incorporate my existing furniture into the new design?

Yes, we can seamlessly blend your existing furniture into the new design. Our goal is to create a fresh look while preserving pieces you love.

4. What should I expect during the design consultation?

During the consultation, we discuss your style, needs, and budget while exploring ideas for your space. This helps us create a clear vision and roadmap for your project.
