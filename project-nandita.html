Nandita Luxury 4BHK Interior Design

home/projects/nandita
Luxury residential

Project information

The Nandita residence is a stunning testament to refined luxury living — a meticulously designed 4BHK home where every room tells a story of elegance, warmth, and purpose. Spread across a generous floor plan, this project called for a seamless blend of opulence and everyday functionality, crafted to reflect the unique personality of its residents.

Our team at 9M Design Studio approached every corner of this home with a deliberate eye for detail — from the grand proportions of the living area to the intimate character of each bedroom suite. Rich textures, curated finishes, and bespoke furniture work in harmony to create interiors that feel both aspirational and deeply liveable.

What defines it

Design highlights

This luxury 4BHK residence showcases the finest elements of contemporary Indian design — where heritage sensibility meets modern sophistication. Every detail was carefully considered to create a home that stands apart.

  • Grand living and dining spaces with double-height proportions and statement lighting.
  • Four luxuriously appointed bedrooms, each with a distinct character and dedicated wardrobe design.
  • A modular kitchen that balances premium aesthetics with high-performance utility.
  • Bespoke joinery and millwork with premium veneers, lacquered finishes, and brass hardware accents.
  • A rich material palette — marble flooring, textured wall panels, velvet upholstery, and natural stone surfaces.
  • Layered ambient, task, and accent lighting to set the perfect mood across all spaces.
  • Seamless indoor-outdoor transition with thoughtfully curated décor and greenery zones.
