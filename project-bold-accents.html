Project information

Bold Accents is a penthouse that wears its personality without apology. This project was born from a client brief that demanded something truly extraordinary — a home that would function as both a private sanctuary and an extension of the client's vibrant, art-loving identity. The result is a space that defies convention, layering bold colour, curated artworks, and luxurious textures to create an interior of irresistible character.

9M Design Studio approached this design with a fearless creative vision — anchoring the scheme in a rich jewel-toned palette of emerald, sapphire, and burnt gold, set against a foundation of dark marble and warm timber. Every furniture piece, every artwork, every decorative object was selected as a considered statement. The three-bedroom suite layout and exclusive rooftop terrace lounge elevate this penthouse to the pinnacle of urban luxury living.

Design highlights

This penthouse is a bold declaration — a home that refuses to whisper when it can speak. Every design choice reinforces a singular vision: expressive, confident, and unmistakably exceptional.

  • Dramatic open living zone with double-height feature wall finished in dark bookmatched marble.
  • Jewel-toned palette: deep emerald velvet seating, sapphire drapes, and burnt gold hardware throughout.
  • Three distinct bedroom suites, each with its own character — maximalist, moody, and art-focused.
  • Curated art collection integrated into the architecture — gallery walls, sculptural installations, and custom murals.
  • Bespoke bar unit with backlit amber glass shelving and a hand-selected spirits collection.
  • Rooftop terrace lounge with outdoor fireplace, modular seating, and panoramic city views.
  • Bespoke lighting design — oversized pendants, wall sconces, and concealed colour-wash LEDs for dramatic effect.
