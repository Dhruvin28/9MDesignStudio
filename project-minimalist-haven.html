Project information

Minimalist Haven is a study in the transformative power of restraint. This 2BHK apartment was designed for clients who understood that true luxury lies not in excess but in the quality of what remains when everything unnecessary has been stripped away. Every surface, every object, every spatial decision in this home was made with a single guiding question: does it belong?

9M Design Studio approached this project with a disciplined rigour that celebrates clean lines, monochromatic serenity, and the extraordinary detail hidden within simplicity. The palette is deliberately restrained — whites, warm greys, pale oak, and a single carefully chosen accent — allowing form, proportion, and light to become the primary design elements. The result is a home that feels calm, expansive, and deliberately alive.

Design highlights

This minimalist apartment demonstrates that considered simplicity is one of the most demanding — and rewarding — design challenges. Every detail earns its place through beauty, function, or both.

  • Seamless spatial flow with no superfluous walls — open plan at its most thoughtful.
  • White and warm grey palette with oak wood accents for warmth and natural texture.
  • Handle-less cabinetry and flush surfaces throughout for uninterrupted visual calm.
  • Two serene bedrooms with custom built-in storage to maintain clutter-free living.
  • A compact but well-considered study corner with integrated shelving and task lighting.
  • Large-format light stone flooring running seamlessly through all living zones.
  • Carefully curated décor: one statement artwork, one indoor plant, one sculptural vase — nothing more.
