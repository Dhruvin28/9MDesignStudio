5bhk ultra luxurious bunglow

Project information

This 5BHK Ultra Luxurious Bunglow is a stunning showcase of luxury living at its finest — a meticulously designed 2500 sqft carpet-area residence that exudes elegance, warmth, and enduring character. Located in the heart of Ahmedabad, this bunglow demanded a design language that honours the generosity of its proportions while delivering an intimately refined experience at every turn.

Our team at 9M Design Studio approached this 5BHK home with deliberate attention to detail — blending premium craftsmanship with thoughtful spatial planning to create interiors that feel as impressive as they are liveable. From grand living areas that set a bold first impression to serene private spaces crafted for quiet comfort, every corner speaks of care, quality, and vision.

Design highlights

This luxurious bunglow interior balances contemporary Indian sensibility with timeless sophistication — every material chosen with purpose, every space designed to delight.

  • Expansive living and dining areas with statement lighting and curated furnishings.
  • Bespoke cabinetry and millwork finished with premium veneers, lacquer, and brass accents.
  • A rich material palette — natural stone flooring, textured wall panels, and plush upholstery.
  • Modular kitchen combining premium aesthetics with functional high-performance design.
  • Layered ambient, task, and accent lighting creating mood-perfect atmospheres across all spaces.
  • Dedicated wardrobe zones and storage solutions tailored to the family's lifestyle.
  • Seamless transitions between interior zones through consistent material and colour storytelling.
